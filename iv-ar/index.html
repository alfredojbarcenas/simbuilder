Setup on a real gel

  1. Put the phone on an overhead stand or gooseneck mount, straight down, 25–30 cm above the gel. An IV needs both hands.
  2. Stick 2 bright dots on the gel, right above each end of the vein. For a curved vein, add 1–3 more dots along it (5 max).
  3. Measure the straight distance between the 2 end dots and set Distance between the 2 end dots.
  4. Any bright, solid color works. If the dots aren't found within a few seconds, the app picks their color itself. You can also tap Set dot color, then tap a dot.
  5. The hand is drawn at the needle end. If it's on the wrong side, tap Flip needle direction.
  6. Learn view: enter at the red X, aim along the dashed line, reach the vein at the green ring. Real view: just the arm.
Top-down camera only: it shows where to puncture and the direction to go, not real needle depth. The side view does the angle math. The arm is a photo anchored to your dots; the vein is drawn along the dots.
