Setup on a real gel

  1. Stick 2 bright green dots on the gel surface, right above each end of the vein.
  2. Measure the distance between them and set Distance between dots.
  3. Hold the phone straight down, about 25–30 cm above the gel, in good light.
  4. If the dots aren't picked up, tap Set dot color, then tap a dot on screen.
  5. Enter at the red X, aim along the dashed line, and reach the vein at the green ring.
Top-down camera only: it shows where to puncture and the direction to go, not real needle depth. The side view does the angle math.
